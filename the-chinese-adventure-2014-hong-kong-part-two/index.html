The Chinese Adventure 2014: Hong Kong – Part Two

The view from Victoria Peak in Hong Kong is famous worldwide, it’s just one of those things you can’t miss when you come over here, and you’re need a pretty decent camera to get the right night time shot (I didn’t quite get what I was hoping for here but I’m still happy with the result). On my second to last day I took the high speed catamaran to the island of Macau, this place is like a mini Las Vegas! Plenty of cool camera opportunities here, crazy majestic buildings, sounds and lights, was a great way to finish what has been an awesome trip! China ~ the size, the culture, the history! ~ this is a truly remarkable country that looks up to no other country, it stands alone in the world, what a great privilege it has been to visit it.
